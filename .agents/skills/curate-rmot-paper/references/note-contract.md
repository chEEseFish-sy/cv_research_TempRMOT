# RMOT paper-note contract

Use Zotero Item Key as immutable identity and the existing Obsidian governance schema.

For an L3 reviewed note, include:

1. One-sentence positioning.
2. Background, research question, and gap.
3. Author claims and contributions.
4. Data, supervision, and evaluation design.
5. Method and concise workflow:
   `input → representation → cross-modal interaction → association/memory → output → loss/evaluation`.
6. Evidence, experiments, ablations, and result interpretation.
7. Limitations and validity threats.
8. Innovation and topic-selection thinking: hidden assumptions, strongest reviewer objection,
   adjacent-work overlap, transferable mechanism, falsifiable hypothesis, minimum experiment,
   and value of a negative result.
9. Reproduction and engineering thinking: code/data/checkpoints/licenses, critical
   hyperparameters, missing details, resource estimate, reproduction order, and failure modes.
10. Relationship to TempRMOT and existing notes.
11. User annotations.
12. Codex inferences, open questions, and an evidence index.

Mark important judgments with evidence class and confidence. Preserve `## 我的思考`
outside managed markers.
